# Purpose

**Include:** Legal matter management, contract repository, case law research, regulatory guidance, outside counsel management, litigation holds, and legal operations workflows.

**Exclude:** Identifiable privileged client communications (store those in secure matter management systems), HR employee matters (unless legal team is directly involved), and general business strategy not requiring legal analysis.
