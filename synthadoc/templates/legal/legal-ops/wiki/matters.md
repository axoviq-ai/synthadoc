# Matters

Active legal matter tracker. Each matter page records: matter ID, type (contract dispute, regulatory inquiry, litigation, M&A, employment), client/business unit, lead counsel (internal and external), status, budget vs. actual spend, key dates, and next actions. Cross-link to [[contracts]], [[case-law]], and [[outside-counsel]].
