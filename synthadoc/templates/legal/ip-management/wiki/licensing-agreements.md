# Licensing Agreements

IP license agreements where this entity is licensor or licensee. Each agreement page records: license type (exclusive/non-exclusive/sole), IP licensed, scope (field of use, territory), royalty rate and base, minimum royalty, term and renewal rights, sublicensing rights, and audit rights. Cross-link to the [[patent-portfolio]] or [[trademark-portfolio]] entries for the licensed IP.
