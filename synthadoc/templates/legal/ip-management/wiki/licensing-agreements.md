# Licensing Agreements

IP license agreements where this entity is licensor or licensee. Each agreement page records:

- **Agreement identity** — agreement title, unique ID, parties (licensor and licensee), execution date
- **IP licensed** — description of the licensed IP (patent numbers, trademark registration numbers, trade secret category, software title)
- **License type** — Exclusive / Non-exclusive / Sole; territory; field of use
- **Financial terms** — upfront fee, royalty rate and royalty base (net sales, units, etc.), minimum annual royalty, payment schedule
- **Term and renewal** — initial term, renewal options, termination triggers
- **Sublicensing rights** — whether sublicensing is permitted and under what conditions
- **Audit rights** — licensor's right to audit licensee's royalty calculations; frequency and notice requirements
- **Representations and warranties** — what each party warrants about the IP and its rights
- **Status** — Active / Expired / Terminated; last royalty payment date and amount
