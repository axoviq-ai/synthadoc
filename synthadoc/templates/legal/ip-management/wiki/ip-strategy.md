# IP Strategy

Organization's intellectual property strategy and portfolio management philosophy. This page records:

- **Strategic objectives** — the top three to five goals the IP portfolio is designed to achieve (freedom to operate, competitive blocking, licensing revenue, defensive portfolio, M&A positioning)
- **Technology focus areas** — which technology domains are prioritized for patent filing
- **Filing criteria** — the threshold test for deciding whether to file: novelty, commercial relevance, prior art strength, cost-benefit, competitive landscape
- **Portfolio targets** — target portfolio size by technology area; ratio of offensive to defensive patents; international filing jurisdictions
- **Licensing strategy** — whether the organization licenses outright, cross-licenses, participates in patent pools, or holds primarily for defense
- **IP budget** — annual IP spend budget; split between prosecution, maintenance, licensing, and enforcement
- **Review cadence** — how often the portfolio is reviewed for alignment with product strategy; who owns the review
