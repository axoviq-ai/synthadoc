# Purpose

**Include:** Regulatory compliance obligations, internal compliance controls, policy library, audit findings, risk register, compliance monitoring, and regulatory change tracking.

**Exclude:** Legal matter management (see legal/legal-ops), IP portfolio management (see legal/ip-management), and operational risk not related to regulatory compliance.
