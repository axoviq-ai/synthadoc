# Regulatory Requirements

Granular obligation inventory extracted from applicable regulations. Each requirement record captures:

- **Requirement identity** — requirement ID, source regulation and section, requirement statement (verbatim or close paraphrase)
- **Regulatory area** — compliance domain (data privacy, financial services, AML, environmental, export, etc.)
- **Obligation type** — what kind of requirement it is: affirmative obligation, prohibition, disclosure, record-keeping, reporting, or training
- **Applicable entity types** — who in the organization must comply (all employees, specific roles, specific products, specific geographies)
- **Implementing policy** — the internal policy or procedure that implements this requirement (link to [[policies]])
- **Control mapping** — which controls demonstrate compliance with this requirement (link to [[controls]])
- **Compliance owner** — department or role accountable for meeting the requirement
- **Current compliance status** — Compliant / Partially Compliant / Non-Compliant; date of last assessment

Cross-link to [[applicable-regulations]] for the source regulation, [[policies]] for implementing policies, [[controls]] for testing evidence, and [[risk-register]] for unmitigated gaps.
