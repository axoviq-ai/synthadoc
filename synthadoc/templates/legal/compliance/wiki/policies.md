# Policies

Policy inventory covering all compliance-relevant internal policies. Each policy record captures:

- **Policy identity** — policy number, title, owner (role and department), version, effective date, next review date
- **Regulatory basis** — the regulation(s), standard(s), or board requirement(s) the policy implements (link to [[applicable-regulations]])
- **Scope** — which business units, employees, third parties, or geographies are covered
- **Core requirements** — the three to five most important things the policy requires; written in plain language for business owners
- **Exceptions process** — how exceptions to the policy are requested, reviewed, and documented
- **Training requirement** — whether employees must complete annual training; last training completion rate
- **Review history** — version history with summary of material changes by version

Cross-link to [[regulatory-requirements]] for the obligations the policy satisfies, [[controls]] for the control activities the policy mandates, and [[audit-findings]] for any gaps between the policy and practice.
