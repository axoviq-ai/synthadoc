# Audit Findings

Log of internal audit findings and regulatory examination findings. Each finding record captures:

- **Finding identity** — finding ID, source (internal audit, external auditor, regulatory examiner, self-assessment), audit or exam name, report date
- **Finding description** — what was observed; what control, process, or policy failed or was absent
- **Root cause** — the underlying reason the finding arose (design gap, operating failure, unclear ownership, insufficient training)
- **Regulatory reference** — the regulation, standard clause, or control objective that was not met
- **Risk rating** — severity assigned by the auditor (High / Medium / Low / Informational)
- **Management response** — how management intends to remediate; whether they accepted or disputed the finding
- **Remediation plan** — specific actions, owners, and target completion dates
- **Status** — Open / In Progress / Closed / Validated; most recent status update date
- **Validation evidence** — what evidence was reviewed to confirm the finding was remediated

Cross-link to [[controls]] for the control that failed, [[risk-register]] if the finding triggers a new or elevated risk, and [[policies]] if a policy gap was identified.
