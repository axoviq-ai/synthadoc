# Risk Register Entry — Intake Form

> **How to use this form**
>
> This template follows the **ISO 31000:2018 Risk Management Guidelines** -- the international standard for risk identification, assessment, and treatment.
> If you already have a risk register in another format, skip this form and ingest your document directly.
>
> Reference: [ISO 31000:2018 Risk Management](https://www.iso.org/iso-31000-risk-management.html)
>
> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `RISK-2026-001-data-breach.md`)
> 2. Fill in all sections
> 3. Run: `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/risk-register/<risk-id>-<short-name>.md -w <wiki>`
>
> Note: One entry per identified risk. Update after each control assessment cycle.
>
> Note: This template is for documentation and knowledge management purposes only — not legal advice. Consult qualified legal counsel for actual legal matters.
