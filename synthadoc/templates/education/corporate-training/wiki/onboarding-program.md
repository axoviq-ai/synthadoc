# Onboarding Program

Structured onboarding program for new employees. Documents: program goals and success criteria, timeline (pre-day-1, day 1, weeks 1-4, months 1-3), required completions by milestone (system access, compliance training, role-specific training, key introductions), buddy and manager checkpoints, and feedback collection approach. Cross-link to role-specific [[new-hire-checklist]] and [[compliance-training]].
