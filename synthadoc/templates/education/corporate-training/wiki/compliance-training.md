# Compliance Training

Required compliance training courses and their completion requirements. Each compliance training record captures:

- **Course identity** — course title, code, regulatory or policy basis (e.g., OSHA 300 log, HIPAA Privacy, Anti-harassment, Export Controls, Code of Conduct)
- **Regulatory requirement** — the law, regulation, or policy that mandates the training; the specific section or citation
- **Applicable audiences** — which employees must complete the training (all employees, specific roles, managers, regulated functions)
- **Completion frequency** — at hire / annual / every 2 years / role change / event-triggered
- **Completion deadline** — how many days from hire or the start of the compliance period
- **Minimum passing standard** — required quiz score; number of attempts permitted
- **Tracking** — how completion is tracked; LMS course code; attestation or certification required
- **Consequence of non-completion** — what happens if an employee does not complete on time (access revoked, disciplinary action, regulatory risk)
