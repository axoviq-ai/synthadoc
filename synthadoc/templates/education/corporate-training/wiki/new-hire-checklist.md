# New Hire Checklist

Role-specific checklists for new employee onboarding milestones. Each checklist record captures:

- **Checklist identity** — role or role family, checklist version, effective date
- **Pre-day-1 items** — actions HR, IT, and the hiring manager must take before the employee's first day (equipment provisioning, system access, workspace setup, welcome communication)
- **Day-1 items** — what happens on the first day: system tours, key introductions, compliance training enrollments, ID badge, parking, tools access verification
- **Week-1 items** — initial training, key stakeholder meetings, first deliverable or observation period, buddy introduction, team lunch or virtual coffee chats
- **30-day milestones** — compliance training completion, initial performance conversation, role-specific orientation completion
- **60-day milestones** — first solo task completion, progress check-in with manager, enrollment in any role-specific certification
- **90-day milestones** — full ramp to independent performance, formal 90-day review, completion of all required training (link to [[compliance-training]])
