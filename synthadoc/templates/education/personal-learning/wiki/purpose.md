# Purpose

**Include:** Personal study notes, book summaries, concept explanations in your own words, mental models, connections between ideas across domains, records of courses or learning projects you are pursuing, and notes from supervisor meetings, classes and study groups.

**Exclude:** Work or professional knowledge that belongs in a domain-specific wiki, social or personal journal content unrelated to learning, and raw uncurated bookmarks without notes.
