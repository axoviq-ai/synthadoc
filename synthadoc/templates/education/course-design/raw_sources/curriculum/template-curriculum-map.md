> **How to use this form**
>
> This template follows the **Backward Design (Wiggins & McTighe "Understanding by Design", 2nd Ed.)** -- the
> curriculum framework that starts with desired learning outcomes and works backward through assessment evidence to instruction.
> If you already have a curriculum document in another format, skip this form and ingest your document directly.
>
> Reference: [Backward Design (Wiggins & McTighe, "Understanding by Design", 2nd Ed.)](https://www.ascd.org/books/understanding-by-design-2nd-edition)
>
> Map the full curriculum before designing individual modules.
>
> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `intro-python-curriculum-map.md`)

## Stage 3 -- Learning Plan (WHERETO)

_(How will we help students achieve the desired results?)_

- **W -- Where and Why**: How will you help students know where the unit is going and why?
- **H -- Hook**: How will you engage students at the start and sustain interest throughout?
- **E -- Equip and Explore**: What experiences, instruction, and resources will equip students to master the goals?
- **R -- Rethink**: What opportunities will students have to revisit, revise, and refine their understanding?
- **E -- Evaluate**: How will students self-assess and reflect on their learning?
- **T -- Tailor**: How will instruction be tailored to varied learner needs, interests, and learning styles?
- **O -- Organize**: How will the sequence be organized for maximum engagement and effectiveness?
