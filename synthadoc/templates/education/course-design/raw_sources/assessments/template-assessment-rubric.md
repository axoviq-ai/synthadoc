>

## Feedback Guidance

_(What feedback should students receive at each performance level?)_

- **4 -- Exceeds**: Acknowledge specific strengths; suggest stretch or extension.
- **3 -- Meets**: Confirm mastery; note one area for continued growth.
- **2 -- Approaching**: Identify the specific gap; provide targeted revision guidance.
- **1 -- Beginning**: Diagnose root cause; recommend additional instruction or support before re-attempt.
