# Facilitation Guides

Instructor and facilitator guides for delivering courses and modules. Each guide captures:

- **Guide identity** — course or module name, guide version, target audience (new facilitators vs. experienced), format (ILT, virtual ILT, blended)
- **Session overview** — total time, room/platform setup requirements, materials checklist, technology requirements
- **Time plan** — minute-by-minute or section-by-section agenda with time allocations; transition language between sections
- **Facilitator notes by section** — what to say and do in each section; suggested talking points (not a word-for-word script); what to watch for in learner responses
- **Discussion questions** — key discussion prompts with sample responses and facilitation tips for drawing out quiet learners
- **Common challenges** — how to handle off-topic questions, technical failures, resistant learners, or sessions running long or short
- **Debrief guide** — how to debrief activities, case studies, and role plays; what learning to surface
- **Accessibility considerations** — how to accommodate learners with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive needs
