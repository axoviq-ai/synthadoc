# Findings

Research findings and results.

Each finding record captures:

- **Finding identity** — finding ID, date, researcher, linked hypothesis (link to [[hypotheses]])
- **Result statement** — precise statement of what was found; in plain language and with statistical values where relevant (e.g. "Group A showed a 23% higher rate than Group B, p < 0.05, d = 0.41")
- **Statistical evidence** — test used, test statistic, p-value, effect size, confidence interval, sample size
- **Interpretation** — what the finding means; how it relates to the hypothesis (supported / partially supported / not supported / unexpected)
- **Boundary conditions** — for whom, under what conditions, and in what context does this finding hold?
- **Replication status** — has this finding been replicated? By whom? In what contexts?
- **Implications** — theoretical implications (what theory does this support or challenge?); practical implications; future research directions
