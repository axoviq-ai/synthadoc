# Purpose

**Include:** Laboratory protocols, standard operating procedures, experimental logs, instrument documentation, reagent inventory, raw data locations, analysis results, and scientific findings for this lab.

**Exclude:** Administrative lab records (grant financials, personnel files), non-science content, and findings from other labs unless directly relevant to replication or comparison.
