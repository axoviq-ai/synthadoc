# Permits

Permit tracker for all development projects.

Each permit record captures:

- **Permit identity** — permit number, permit type (demolition / grading / shoring / foundation / building / electrical / plumbing / mechanical / fire / certificate of occupancy), project, issuing authority (city, county, or state agency)
- **Application and fees** — submission date, submitted by (GC or owner), application fee paid, plan check fee paid, plan review comments received
- **Issuance** — issuance date, permit expiration date, permitted scope (square footage, construction value)
- **Conditions of approval** — specific conditions from the building department, fire marshal, or health department; compliance documentation required
- **Inspection sign-offs** — which inspections are required for this permit; sign-off status (see [[inspections]])
- **Renewal and expiration** — expiry date, whether an extension has been filed, extension fee and approval date
