# Permits

Permit tracker for all development projects. Each permit entry records: permit number, permit type (grading, foundation, framing, MEP, certificate of occupancy), project, issuing authority (city, county, state), submission date, submission fees, issuance date, permit expiry, conditions of approval, and inspection sign-off status. Flag expired or expiring permits requiring renewal.
