# Purpose

**Include:** Real estate development project documentation: entitlements, zoning analysis, permits, construction schedules, contractor management, design documents, inspection records, and change orders.

**Exclude:** Investment underwriting for stabilized properties (see real-estate/investment), ongoing property management after stabilization (see real-estate/property-management), and land acquisition analysis pre-entitlement.
