# Contractors

General contractor and subcontractor profiles for all development projects. Each contractor page records: company name, license number and state, insurance coverage (GL, workers comp, umbrella), bonding capacity, contract type (lump sum/GMP/time-and-materials), contract value, project(s) assigned, key contact, and performance history. Cross-link to [[change-orders]] and [[inspections]] associated with each contractor.
