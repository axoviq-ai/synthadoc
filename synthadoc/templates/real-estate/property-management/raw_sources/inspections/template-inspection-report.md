> **How to use this form**
>
> This template follows the **IREM Property Inspection Standards + BOMA (Building Owners and Managers Association) guidelines** -- the standard framework for documenting property condition at move-in, move-out, and periodic inspections.
> If you already have an inspection form in another format, skip this form and ingest your document directly.
>

> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `maple-apts-unit-204-move-in-2026-09-01.md`)
> 2. Fill in all sections
> 3. Run: `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/inspections/<filename>.md -w <wiki>`
>
> Complete at move-in, move-out, and at least annually for each unit/area. Photos should be labeled and stored with this report.
