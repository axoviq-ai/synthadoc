# Leases

Lease abstract library. Each lease page records: tenant name, property address, unit/suite, lease type (gross/net/NNN), lease commencement date, lease expiration date, base rent and escalation schedule, renewal and expansion options, tenant improvement allowance, permitted use, and key tenant obligations (maintenance, insurance, CAM). Flag leases expiring within 12 months and document the renewal strategy.
