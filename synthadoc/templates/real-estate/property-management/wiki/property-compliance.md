# Property Compliance

Regulatory compliance obligations for all managed properties.

Each compliance record captures:

- **Property identity** — property address, property type, year built, number of units
- **Certificate of occupancy** — current CO number, issue date, occupancy classification, any temporary COs outstanding
- **Life safety systems** — fire sprinkler inspection (last date, next due, result), fire alarm test (last date, next due, result), elevator annual inspection (certification date), emergency lighting (last test)
- **Local habitability requirements** — lead paint disclosure (pre-1978 properties), asbestos survey and management plan, mold disclosure, bed bug addendum required by jurisdiction
- **Energy and utilities compliance** — energy benchmarking requirement (EPA ENERGY STAR / local ordinance), utility meter compliance, green building certification status
- **ADA / accessibility** — path of travel compliance status, accommodation request history, pending modifications
- **Landlord-tenant law compliance** — required disclosures by jurisdiction, notice requirements for entry, habitability repair timeline obligations, rent control applicability
- **Inspection calendar** — all required inspections with due dates and current status
