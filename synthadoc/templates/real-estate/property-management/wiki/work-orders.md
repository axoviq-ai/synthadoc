# Work Orders

Maintenance work order tracker. Each work order records: WO number, property, unit/area, submission date, tenant or staff submitter, issue description, priority (emergency/urgent/routine), assigned vendor or maintenance technician, estimated completion date, actual completion date, resolution description, and total cost. Cross-link to [[vendors]] and [[tenants]].
