## Target Return Metrics

*Minimum thresholds — any acquisition model should meet or exceed these.*

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **IRR (levered)** | % | Minimum over the full hold period; use XIRR |
| **Equity Multiple (EM)** | × | Total return ÷ cash invested |
| **Average cash-on-cash** | % | Average annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ equity |
| **DSCR at acquisition** | × | NOI ÷ annual debt service; typically ≥ 1.20 |
| **Cap rate at purchase** | % | Minimum going-in cap rate |
| **GRM at purchase** | × | Max gross rent multiplier you will underwrite |

*Definitions follow NCREIF/CRE convention. IRR via XIRR on dated cash flows.*
