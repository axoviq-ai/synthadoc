# Financial Models

Property-level financial models for underwriting and ongoing monitoring. Each model page records: property address, model date, key assumptions (vacancy, rent growth, expense ratio, cap rate, exit cap rate, hold period), debt terms, projected NOI by year, projected IRR and equity multiple, sensitivity analysis summary, and actual-vs-underwriting comparison for operating properties.
