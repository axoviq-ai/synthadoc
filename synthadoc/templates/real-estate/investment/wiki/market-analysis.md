# Market Analysis

Submarket research and market condition tracking for the portfolio's primary investment areas. Populate by ingesting market reports from Zillow Research, Redfin Data Center, Realtor.com Research, local MLS board publications, or broker reports (JLL, CBRE, Marcus & Millichap).

Each submarket analysis page captures:

- **Geography** — city, neighbourhood, zip codes, and market boundaries
- **Property type focus** — residential (SFH, condo, multifamily), commercial, industrial
- **Vacancy rate** — current and trend (quarter-over-quarter, year-over-year)
- **Average asking rent** — and rent growth rate
- **Median sale price** — and price appreciation trend
- **Cap rate range** — current market cap rates by asset class
- **Gross rent multiplier (GRM)** — market-level GRM for benchmarking acquisitions
- **Supply pipeline** — units or sqft under construction; expected delivery dates
- **Absorption rate** — months of supply; demand vs. new inventory
- **Employment drivers** — major employers, job growth, migration trends
- **Demand indicators** — population growth, household formation, permit activity
- **Recent comparable sales** — 3–5 closed transactions relevant to portfolio assets
- **Report source and date** — data currency is critical; note source and publication date
