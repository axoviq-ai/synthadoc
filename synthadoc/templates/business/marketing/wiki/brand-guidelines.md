# Brand Guidelines

Brand identity reference: logo usage rules (clear space, minimum size, prohibited modifications), primary and secondary color palette (hex, RGB, CMYK values), typography (heading and body typefaces with weights and sizes), photography and imagery style, and tone-of-voice guide (brand personality attributes, do/don't examples). This page is the authoritative reference — any deviation requires documented approval.
