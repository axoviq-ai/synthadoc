# Content Strategy

Content marketing strategy and editorial policy. Populate by ingesting your content strategy document and editorial calendar.

Each content strategy record captures:

- **Content mission** — who the content serves, what topics the brand has authority to publish on, what the content is designed to do (educate / build trust / generate leads / retain customers)
- **Content pillars** — 3–5 topical pillars that every content piece should connect to; sub-topics under each pillar; keyword themes per pillar
- **Audience and format map** — which content formats (long-form / video / podcast / social / email) serve which audiences at which buying stages
- **Editorial standards** — fact-checking requirements, attribution standards, expert review process, brand voice application rules
- **Distribution channels** — primary and secondary channels for each content type, posting frequency targets, amplification workflow (email blast, social promotion, paid boost threshold)
- **SEO integration** — how content addresses target keyword clusters (link to [[seo]]), internal linking strategy, content refresh cadence
- **Performance benchmarks** — traffic, engagement rate, lead gen rate, and backlink targets by content type
