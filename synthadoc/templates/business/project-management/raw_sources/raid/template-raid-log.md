# RAID Log: [Project Name]

> **How to use this form**
>
> This template follows the **PMI/PMBOK risk and issue management standard** extended with
> the RAID framework (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies) — the most widely adopted
> format for enterprise project tracking.
>
> Reference: PMI PMBOK Guide 6th Ed., Chapter 11 (Risk Management)
>
> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `erp-migration-raid-log.md`)
> 2. Add entries as risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies are identified
> 3. Run: `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/raid/erp-migration-raid-log.md -w <wiki>`
>
> Alternatively, ingest your existing RAID log directly -- Excel, CSV, or any format is
> supported. Place it in `raw_sources/raid/` and run:
> `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/raid/<your-file>.<ext> -w <wiki>`
>
> Re-ingest whenever entries are added, updated, or closed.
