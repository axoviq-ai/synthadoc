# Stakeholder Analysis: [Project Name]

> **How to use this form**
>
> This template uses **Mendelow's Power/Interest Grid** -- the most widely adopted stakeholder
> analysis framework in project management, used in PMI PMBOK, PRINCE2, and most enterprise
> PM methodologies. Classify each stakeholder by their power over the project and their interest
> in its outcome, then tailor your engagement strategy to their quadrant.
>
> Reference: Mendelow, A.L. (1984); PMI PMBOK Guide 6th Ed., Section 13.1
>
> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `erp-migration-stakeholders.md`)
> 2. Add one row per stakeholder in the Stakeholder Register table
> 3. Run: `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/stakeholders/erp-migration-stakeholders.md -w <wiki>`
>
> Alternatively, ingest your existing stakeholder analysis document directly -- any format is
> supported. Place it in `raw_sources/stakeholders/` and run:
> `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/stakeholders/<your-file>.<ext> -w <wiki>`
>
> Re-ingest when stakeholder roles, attitudes, or engagement strategy changes.
