## First steps checklist

- [ ] **Create a project charter** -- use our template or bring your own:
  - Template: copy `raw_sources/charters/template-project-charter.md`, rename it
    (e.g. `erp-migration-q4-2026.md`), fill in all sections, get sponsor sign-off, then:
    ```
    synthadoc ingest raw_sources/charters/<project-name>.md -w <wiki>
    ```
  - Own doc: place your existing charter (PDF, Word, Markdown) in `raw_sources/charters/` and ingest it.
  Populates [[project-charters]] and [[projects]].

- [ ] **Document your kickoff meeting** -- use our template or bring your own:
  - Template: copy `raw_sources/kickoff/template-project-kickoff.md`, rename it
    (e.g. `erp-migration-kickoff-2026-10-01.md`), fill in attendees, decisions, and action items, then:
    ```
    synthadoc ingest raw_sources/kickoff/<project-name>-kickoff.md -w <wiki>
    ```
  - Own doc: place your existing kickoff notes in `raw_sources/kickoff/` and ingest them.
  Populates [[projects]] with scope confirmation, team assignments, and open questions.

- [ ] **Build a RAID log** -- use our template or bring your own:
  - Template: copy `raw_sources/raid/template-raid-log.md`, rename it, add entries, then:
    ```
    synthadoc ingest raw_sources/raid/<project-name>-raid-log.md -w <wiki>
    ```
  - Own doc: place your existing RAID log (Excel, CSV, Markdown) in `raw_sources/raid/` and ingest it.
  Populates [[raid-log]].

- [ ] **Build a stakeholder analysis** -- use our template or bring your own:
  - Template: copy `raw_sources/stakeholders/template-stakeholder-analysis.md`, rename it,
    fill in one row per stakeholder, then:
    ```
    synthadoc ingest raw_sources/stakeholders/<project-name>-stakeholders.md -w <wiki>
    ```
  - Own doc: place your existing stakeholder analysis in `raw_sources/stakeholders/` and ingest it.
  Populates [[stakeholder-map]].

- [ ] **Review and promote candidates** -- all ingested pages land in `candidates/` for review.
  In Obsidian: open the command palette (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and run
  **"Synthadoc: Candidates: review candidate pages..."** to promote or discard each page.
  Or from CLI: `synthadoc candidates promote --all -w <wiki>`

- [ ] **Run scaffold to build the index**
  ```
  synthadoc scaffold -w <wiki>
  ```
