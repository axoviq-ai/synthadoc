# Purpose

**Include:** Project charters, project schedules, RAID logs, stakeholder maps, status reports, decision logs, and retrospectives for all managed projects.

**Exclude:** Day-to-day task tracking in Jira or similar tools, individual contributor workload management, and operational BAU activities that do not meet the project definition threshold.
