# RAID Log

Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies tracker for all projects. Each entry records: ID, category (R/A/I/D), description, project, owner, date identified, status (open/closed), probability (for risks), impact (high/medium/low), mitigation or action plan, and target resolution date. Review the RAID log at every project status meeting and update status after each review.
