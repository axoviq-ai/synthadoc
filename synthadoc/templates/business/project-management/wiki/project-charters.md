# Project Charters

Signed project charters for all authorized projects.

Each project charter captures:

- **Charter identity** -- project name, ID, sponsor, PM, PM authority level, start/end dates, status
- **Problem statement and objective** -- the business problem or opportunity, a measurable time-bound success statement, and explicit success criteria
- **High-level requirements** -- regulatory, technical, user-facing, and integration constraints the outcome must satisfy
- **Scope** -- in-scope deliverables and explicitly out-of-scope items
- **Deliverables** -- list with description, due date, and acceptance criteria for each
- **Stakeholders** -- name, role, interest level, communication need
- **Budget** -- approved total, breakdown (labor / tools / vendors / contingency), contingency reserve
- **Key milestones** -- milestone name and target date
- **Top risks** -- top 3-5 risks with likelihood, impact, and mitigation
- **Assumptions and constraints** -- what the plan assumes, what limits the project
- **Project approval requirements** -- who decides success, deliverable sign-off process, exit criteria
- **Approvals** -- sponsor and PM signatures
