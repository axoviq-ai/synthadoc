# Roadmap

Product roadmap — what's planned, in progress, and shipped by theme and time horizon.

Each roadmap record captures:

- **Roadmap horizon** — Now (current quarter) / Next (next 1–2 quarters) / Later (6+ months); confidence levels drop as horizon extends
- **Themes** — strategic themes or bets that organize the roadmap (e.g. "Reduce Time to Value", "Enterprise Readiness", "Platform Extensibility")
- **Initiatives under each theme** — initiative name, owning team, target outcome, customer segment impacted
- **Feature items under each initiative** — feature name (link to [[prds]]), priority rank, rough sizing (S/M/L/XL), status
- **Dependencies** — cross-team or external dependencies that could affect the roadmap
- **What's not on the roadmap** — explicitly declined ideas and why (helps prevent re-litigating decisions)
- **Last updated** — date and who approved the current roadmap state

Cross-link to [[prds]], [[okrs]], [[customer-research]], and [[product-metrics]].
