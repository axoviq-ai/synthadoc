# Customer Research

Research study library. Each study page records: research method (user interview, usability test, survey, diary study, A/B test), research question(s), participant count and recruitment criteria, date conducted, researcher, key findings ranked by frequency and severity, and recommended actions. Cross-link to [[prds]] or [[roadmap]] items that were informed by each study.
