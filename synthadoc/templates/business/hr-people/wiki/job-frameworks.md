# Job Frameworks

Job family and leveling framework. Each job family page defines: scope (what work this family covers), career levels (IC1 through Staff/Principal or equivalent), scope and impact expectations at each level, competency expectations (technical and behavioral) with observable indicators, typical next roles, and the promotion criteria between levels. Cross-link to [[compensation]] for band information at each level.
