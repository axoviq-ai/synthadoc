# Performance Management

Performance review process: review cycle calendar (mid-year and annual dates), self-assessment process, manager assessment framework, 360 feedback process (if applicable), rating scale definitions, calibration process (pre-calibration and calibration session), and compensation action timing. Cross-link to [[job-frameworks]] for level-specific expectations used in assessment.
