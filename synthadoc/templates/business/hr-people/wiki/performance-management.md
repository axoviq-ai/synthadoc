# Performance Management

Performance review process, rating scales, and calibration guidelines. Populate by ingesting your performance management policy and review templates.

Each performance management record captures:

- **Review cycle** — frequency (annual / bi-annual / quarterly check-ins), timeline (when reviews open, when feedback is due, when calibration sessions happen, when results are shared)
- **Rating scale** — scale definition (e.g. 1–5 or Exceptional/Exceeds/Meets/Below/Unsatisfactory), what each rating means operationally, forced distribution policy (if any)
- **Goal-setting process** — when goals are set, what format (OKR / SMART / custom), how goals are aligned to company objectives, what percentage of rating is tied to goal attainment vs. competencies
- **360-degree feedback** — whether peers/reports give input, how peer feedback is weighted, anonymity policy
- **Calibration process** — who participates in calibration, calibration facilitator role, how managers defend ratings, how cross-team consistency is ensured
- **PIP process** — criteria for placing someone on a performance improvement plan, PIP structure (duration, check-ins, success criteria), documentation requirements
- **Documentation requirements** — what must be written down, retention period, employee access rights
