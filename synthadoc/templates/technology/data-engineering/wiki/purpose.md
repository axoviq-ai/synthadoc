# Purpose

**Include:** Data pipeline documentation, dataset catalog, schema registry, data quality rules, data lineage, data governance policies, and ETL/ELT processes managed by the data engineering team.

**Exclude:** Business intelligence reports (store those in BI tooling), ML model training (see technology/ai-ml), and application database schemas managed by product engineering.
