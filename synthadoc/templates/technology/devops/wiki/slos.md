# SLOs — Service Level Objectives

SLI and SLO definitions for each service. Each entry records: service name, SLI type (availability, latency, error rate), SLO target (e.g., 99.9% over 30 days), measurement window, data source, and current error budget burn rate. Cross-link to the service's alert rules and on-call runbook.
