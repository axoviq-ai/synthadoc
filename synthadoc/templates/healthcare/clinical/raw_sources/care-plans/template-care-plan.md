# Care Plan — Template

> **How to use this form**
>
> This template follows the **Joint Commission Care Planning Standards (PC.01.02.01) + NANDA-I nursing diagnoses** -- the regulatory and clinical standard for individualized inpatient and outpatient care planning. If you already have a care plan in another format, skip this form and ingest your document directly.
>
> Reference: [The Joint Commission Standards](https://www.jointcommission.org/standards/)
>
> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `PT-2026-0042-2026-09-08.md`)
> 2. Fill in all sections
> 3. Run: `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/care-plans/<filename>.md -w <wiki>`
>
> **Note:** This template is for documentation purposes — always follow institutional clinical protocols and regulatory requirements for actual patient care or submissions.
> Complete within 24h of admission or at first outpatient encounter.
