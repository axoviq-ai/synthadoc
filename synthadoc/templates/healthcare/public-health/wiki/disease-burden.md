# Disease Burden

Morbidity and mortality estimates by condition. Each condition page records:

- **Condition identity** — condition name, ICD-10 code, disease category
- **Incidence rate** — new cases per 100,000 per year; source and year; trend direction
- **Prevalence** — proportion of population currently affected; source and year
- **Mortality rate** — deaths per 100,000 (crude and age-standardized); case fatality rate if relevant
- **DALYs** — disability-adjusted life years lost (Global Burden of Disease estimate if available)
- **Demographic breakdown** — disease distribution by age, sex, race/ethnicity; highest-burden populations
- **Geographic variation** — regional or country-level differences; rural vs. urban if relevant
- **Trends** — direction of change over the past 10–20 years; factors driving increase or decrease
- **Data quality limitations** — under-reporting concerns; case definition consistency; data lag

Cross-link to [[surveillance]] for the data systems that track each condition, [[health-programs]] for interventions targeting the condition, and [[health-equity]] for burden disparities by population.
