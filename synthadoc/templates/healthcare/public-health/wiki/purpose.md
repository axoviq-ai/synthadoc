# Purpose

**Include:** Epidemiological data, disease surveillance, population health metrics, public health interventions, health policy analysis, program evaluation, and health equity research.

**Exclude:** Individual patient clinical care, pharmaceutical development, hospital operations, and general health education content not grounded in population-level evidence.
