> **How to use this form**
>
> This template follows the **ICH E3 Clinical Study Report Structure + ClinicalTrials.gov registration format** -- the international standard for summarising clinical trial design, conduct, and results. If you already have a clinical study report or trial summary in another format, skip this form and ingest your document directly.
>
> Reference: [ICH E3 Clinical Study Report](https://www.ich.org/page/e3)
>
> 1. Copy this file and rename it (e.g. `AXV-101-phase2-summary.md`)
> 2. Fill in all sections
> 3. Run: `synthadoc ingest raw_sources/clinical-trials/<filename>.md -w <wiki>`
