# Safety (Pharmacovigilance)

Drug safety database and pharmacovigilance records. Each safety record captures:

- **Safety signal** — the adverse event or drug–drug interaction of interest; MedDRA preferred term and system organ class
- **Compound** — which compound(s) this safety finding applies to (link to [[compounds]])
- **Source** — clinical trial, spontaneous report, literature, regulatory signal, post-marketing surveillance
- **Incidence** — event rate in clinical trials (treatment arm vs. comparator); spontaneous reporting rate
- **Seriousness** — whether the event meets ICH E2A serious criteria (fatal, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability)
- **Causality assessment** — investigator-assessed and sponsor-assessed causality; strength of evidence
- **Current label status** — whether the event appears in the current prescribing information; any boxed warning, contraindication, or warning related to this event
- **Risk mitigation** — REMS requirements, Dear HCP letters, patient medication guide, any other risk minimization measures

Cross-link to [[compounds]] for the compound profile, [[clinical-trials]] for the trial where the event was observed, [[regulatory-submissions]] for IND safety reports and label negotiations, and [[protocols]] for DSMB stopping rule context.
