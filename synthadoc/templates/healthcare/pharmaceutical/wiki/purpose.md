# Purpose

**Include:** Drug development programs, clinical trial protocols, regulatory submissions, safety monitoring, CMC documentation, and pharmacology data for pharmaceutical, biotech, or medical device development.

**Exclude:** Commercial sales data, managed care contracting, and clinical practice guidelines for prescribers (see healthcare/clinical).
