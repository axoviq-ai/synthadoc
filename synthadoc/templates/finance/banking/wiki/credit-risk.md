# Credit Risk

Credit policy, underwriting standards, and portfolio risk management.

Each credit risk record captures:

- **Loan approval authority matrix** — who can approve loans up to what dollar amount and risk rating (officer, senior officer, loan committee, board)
- **Risk rating definitions** — internal risk grades (e.g. 1–9 scale), Pass/Watch/Special Mention/Substandard/Doubtful/Loss definitions; mapping to regulatory classification
- **Underwriting standards by product** — maximum LTV, minimum FICO, maximum DTI, collateral requirements, guaranty standards per product type
- **Portfolio concentration limits** — CRE concentration as % of capital (300% / 100% HVCRE thresholds), geographic concentration, industry concentration
- **CECL / allowance methodology** — model type (discounted cash flow, loss rate, PD×LGD), historical loss period, Q-factors, qualitative adjustments, effective date of CECL adoption
- **Stress testing** — assumptions, scenarios (baseline / adverse / severely adverse), results, and action thresholds
- **Charge-off and recovery** — charge-off triggers by loan type, recovery procedures, aging triggers for non-accrual placement
