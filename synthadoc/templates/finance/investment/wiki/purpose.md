# Purpose

**Include:** Investment research covering public equities, private equity, venture capital, credit instruments, and alternative assets. Company profiles, deal analysis, financial models, valuation work, earnings notes, and portfolio-level tracking belong here.

**Exclude:** Personal banking, mortgage origination, retail brokerage how-to guides, tax preparation, and general financial literacy. Those belong in separate wikis under the relevant finance domain.
