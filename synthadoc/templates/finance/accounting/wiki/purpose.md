# Purpose

**Include:** Financial reporting under GAAP or IFRS, month-end and year-end close processes, account reconciliations, internal controls documentation, audit preparation and findings, tax compliance, and accounting policy memos.

**Exclude:** Investment analysis, financial planning and budgeting (FP&A), operational management, and HR payroll details not related to accounting entries.
