# Purpose

**Include:** Manufacturing process specifications, quality control procedures, defect tracking and root cause analysis, control plans, inspection methods, calibration records, and quality standards compliance documentation.

**Exclude:** Sales orders and production scheduling, HR and personnel records, financial cost tracking, and engineering design files (CAD).
