# Quality Standards

Quality management standards and certifications applicable to the organization. Each standard record captures:

- **Standard identity** — standard name and number (ISO 9001:2015, IATF 16949:2016, AS9100 Rev D, ISO 13485, NADCAP), issuing body, current edition
- **Applicability** — which sites, product lines, or customers require this certification; why the standard applies
- **Certification status** — Certified / Registered / Compliant (no cert) / In pursuit; certificate number; certifying body (registrar)
- **Scope of certification** — the official scope statement on the certificate; what is excluded from scope
- **Certificate expiry and surveillance audits** — expiry date; surveillance audit schedule (typically annual for ISO); next audit date
- **Key requirements summary** — the five to ten most operationally significant clauses for our business; plain language interpretation
- **Gap assessment** — known gaps relative to the current standard version; planned remediation
- **Customer specific requirements** — extensions to the standard required by specific customers (link to [[customer-requirements]])
