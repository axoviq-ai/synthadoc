# Process Specifications

Manufacturing process specifications indexed by product line and operation. Each spec page records: document number, revision level, effective date, approver, scope, raw material requirements, process parameters (temperature, pressure, speed, time), critical-to-quality characteristics, and related control plan. Cross-link to [[inspection-procedures]] and [[gauges]] used in the process.
