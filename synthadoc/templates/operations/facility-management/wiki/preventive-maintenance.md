# Preventive Maintenance

PM schedule for all facility assets. Each PM task page records: asset ID, task description, required skills and tools, parts typically consumed, estimated labor hours, frequency (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual), last completed date, and next due date. Cross-link to [[assets]] and [[vendor-contracts]] for outsourced tasks.
