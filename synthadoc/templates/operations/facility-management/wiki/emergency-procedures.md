# Emergency Procedures

Emergency response plans and procedures for the facility. Each procedure record captures:

- **Emergency type** — the specific emergency: fire, chemical spill, medical emergency, power outage, flood, active threat, HVAC failure, elevator entrapment
- **Trigger criteria** — conditions that activate this procedure; alarm or notification source
- **Immediate actions** — the first five actions employees must take (in order); who is notified first
- **Evacuation** — assembly point locations; evacuation routes by floor and area; responsibilities of floor wardens
- **Emergency contacts** — 911; building security; facility manager; maintenance on-call; utilities emergency lines
- **Roles and responsibilities** — who is the incident commander; who controls head count; who manages communications with first responders
- **Specialized response steps** — spill containment procedures, AED locations, fire suppression system reset, utility shutoff locations
- **Recovery** — who authorizes re-entry; steps required before returning to normal operations; incident documentation requirements
