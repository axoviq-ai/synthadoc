# Assets

Facility asset register. Each asset entry records: asset ID, asset type (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire suppression, elevator, production equipment), description, building/floor/room location, manufacturer, model, serial number, installation date, warranty expiry, replacement cost, and current condition (good/fair/poor). Cross-link to [[preventive-maintenance]] schedule and [[work-orders]] history.
