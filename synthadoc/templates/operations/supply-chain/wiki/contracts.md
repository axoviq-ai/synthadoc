# Contracts

Supply and vendor contracts in the procurement portfolio. Each contract record captures:

- **Contract identity** — contract ID, supplier name (link to [[suppliers]]), agreement type (master supply agreement, blanket purchase order, long-term agreement, spot purchase)
- **Materials or services covered** — specific parts, materials, or services; part numbers if applicable
- **Term** — effective date, expiry date, auto-renewal clause and notice period
- **Pricing** — pricing schedule, unit prices, volume breaks, price change mechanism (annual CPI adjustment, quarterly index review)
- **Volume commitments** — minimum purchase commitment; take-or-pay provisions; forecast obligations
- **Quality and delivery terms** — AQL standards, delivery lead time, packaging requirements, on-time delivery penalty
- **Change-of-control** — assignment rights; whether the agreement continues through supplier M&A
- **Dispute resolution** — governing law, escalation path, arbitration or litigation clause

Cross-link to [[suppliers]] for the supplier profile, [[materials]] for the parts covered, [[procurement-procedures]] for the approval matrix that governs new contracts, and [[vendor-scorecards]] for the supplier performance data used in contract renewal decisions.
