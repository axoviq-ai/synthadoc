# Suppliers

Approved supplier register. Each supplier page records: company name, category (raw material, component, MRO, logistics), supply chain tier (Tier 1/2/3), primary contact, lead time, payment terms, quality certification (ISO 9001, IATF, AS9100), last audit date and result, and critical parts supplied. Flag sole-source suppliers and document the supply disruption contingency plan.
