# Vendor Scorecards

Periodic supplier performance scorecards. Each scorecard record captures:

- **Supplier** — supplier name and code (link to [[suppliers]]); period covered (monthly, quarterly)
- **Quality score** — incoming DPPM (defects per million); returns and rejections; corrective action responsiveness; weight in overall score
- **Delivery score** — on-time delivery rate (by line or by order); lead time adherence; short-ship or over-ship incidents; weight in overall score
- **Cost/commercial score** — invoice accuracy rate; price variance vs. PO; cost reduction initiatives delivered; weight in overall score
- **Responsiveness / service score** — response time to NCRs; RFQ response time; proactive communication; capacity flexibility
- **Overall score and rating** — weighted total score; rating tier: Preferred / Approved / Conditional / Under Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) / Disqualified
- **Trend** — score vs. prior period; direction of change
- **Actions** — any PIP initiated; target improvements agreed with supplier; review date

Cross-link to [[suppliers]] for the supplier profile, [[contracts]] for any performance clauses that trigger review, and [[procurement-procedures]] for how scorecard results affect purchasing decisions and new supplier onboarding.
