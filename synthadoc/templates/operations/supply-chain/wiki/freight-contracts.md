# Freight Contracts

Carrier and freight broker agreements and lane rate schedules. Each freight contract record captures:

- **Contract identity** — contract ID, carrier or broker name, mode (TL, LTL, parcel, air, ocean, intermodal), contract type (spot, dedicated, master carrier agreement)
- **Lanes and rates** — origin–destination pairs covered; per-mile or per-shipment rate; fuel surcharge mechanism; accessorial fee schedule
- **Service standards** — transit time by lane; service level: guaranteed, standard, or best-effort; claims rate history
- **Capacity commitments** — tender acceptance rate committed by the carrier; minimum volume committed by shipper
- **Contract term** — effective date, expiry, rate review schedule
- **Carrier qualifications** — DOT number, MC number, insurance certificate on file, safety rating, cargo liability limits
- **Incoterms** — delivery terms for international shipments (EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP, etc.) if applicable; customs broker arrangement
